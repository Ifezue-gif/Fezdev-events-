About FEZDEV

Company overview

FEZDEV is the company behind two complementary products: Fezdev Meetings for private sessions and Fezdev Events for public listings. Together, they help organizations bring people together and coordinate their work.

Fezdev Meetings

A scheduling service for private sessions. Hosts manage availability, book open time slots, and share calendar invitations with participants.

Fezdev Events

An event platform for public conferences, hackathons, and workshops. Organizers publish event details so audiences can discover and take part.
