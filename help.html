Getting started

FEZDEV brings together two complementary products. Fezdev Meetings is for private sessions booked into a time slot. Fezdev Events is for public listings such as conferences and hackathons. Use the menu to move between the products, company information, and Help.

Scheduling meetings

  1. Open the Meetings page and choose Schedule a meeting.
  2. Enter a title and choose 15, 30 or 60 minutes.
  3. Pick a date, then select one of the open time slots.
  4. Choose Google Meet or Zoom and add participant emails.
  5. Select Schedule meeting. A calendar file downloads and a summary opens.

Slots that are already booked or in the past are greyed out and cannot be selected.

Availability and time zones

The host availability panel sets working days, working hours, the gap between meetings, and the zone those hours belong to. Slots are shown in the time zone selected on the form, which defaults to the zone your browser reports. Change it to book for someone in another region.

Publishing events

  1. Open Publish an event from the homepage.
  2. Add the event name, type, track and format (in person, online or hybrid).
  3. Set the start and end in the event's time zone, then add the venue or streaming link.
  4. Write a public description and choose whether to publish the speakers directory.
  5. Select Publish event. It appears under Newly published on the homepage.

Calendar files

Every meeting and event can be exported as an .ics file. Open it to add the entry to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook. Use Add to calendar on any saved item to download it again.

Saved data

Meetings, events and availability are saved in this browser, so they stay after a refresh. They do not sync to other devices.

DevHorizon26

Choose DevHorizon26 from the featured events section. It opens the separate multi-file project in the devhorizon26 folder.

Important: this is a front-end prototype. Real email delivery, sign-in, shared database storage, and automatic Google Meet or Zoom link creation require a backend and the relevant service APIs.
